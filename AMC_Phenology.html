AMC Phenology

Checks how much phenology data the iNaturalist project Flowers and Fauna along the Appalachian Trail Corridor holds (the Appalachian Mountain Club’s A.T. Seasons project). For each candidate plant it counts records, flowering, fruiting and bud annotations, how many records are annotated at all, where flowering records fall along the trail and in which years and months. Find trail-wide species ranks every plant or animal in the project by how evenly its records cover the trail. Counts come live from iNaturalist (research grade and needs ID, the “verifiable” records), one species at a time, so a full run takes a few minutes.

Find trail-wide species ranks every species of the kind you pick (plants by flowering records; animals, birds or insects by all records; or animals by records annotated adult) by its records in its weakest third of the trail (south of 38°N, 38–42°N, north of 42°N): the higher, the more continuously it is recorded along the whole trail. Four requests, a few seconds. Flowering south: flowering records south of 38°N (Georgia to southern Virginia). Flowering north: north of 42°N (Massachusetts to Maine). Month bars show flowering records, January to December.

Download records for one species

Every record of a species in the project, with all of its attributes: date and time, location and accuracy, quality grade, identifications, observer, annotations (flowers and fruits, leaves, life stage and others), observation fields, photos and the link to the record.
