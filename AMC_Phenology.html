AMC Phenology

Checks how much phenology data the iNaturalist project Flowers and Fauna along the Appalachian Trail Corridor holds (the Appalachian Mountain Club’s A.T. Seasons project). For each of the 34 AMC focal wildflower species (spring ephemerals, summer-green and evergreen species) it counts records, flowering, fruiting and bud annotations, how many records are annotated at all, where flowering records fall along the trail and in which years and months. Counts come live from iNaturalist (research grade and needs ID, the “verifiable” records), one species at a time, so a full run takes a few minutes.

Flowering south: flowering records south of 38°N (Georgia to southern Virginia). Flowering north: north of 42°N (Massachusetts to Maine). Month bars show flowering records, January to December.

Download records

Export all AMC focal species downloads every record of all 34 focal species in one file, each row tagged with the species’ functional group, A.T.-wide and rich-woods flags and family, with a Species sheet of totals (Excel). Download records does the same for one species you type. Each record comes with all of its attributes: date and time, location and accuracy, quality grade, identifications, observer, annotations (flowers and fruits, leaves, life stage and others), observation fields, photos and the link to the record.
