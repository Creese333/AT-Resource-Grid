Phenology data check

Counts how many iNaturalist records in the Appalachian National Scenic Trail – HUC10 project carry a flowering or fruiting annotation, for each candidate species. It asks iNaturalist directly from this browser, about one small request a second (roughly two minutes for the list).

wave: 150+ flowering and fruiting records, enough to animate the season up the trail. shift: 30+ flowering records in both 2016–2020 and 2021–2025. Month bars show flowering records, January to December.
