Phenology data check

Counts how many iNaturalist records in the Appalachian National Scenic Trail – HUC10 project carry a flowering or fruiting annotation, for each candidate species: northern and southern indicators and the A.T. Seasons focal plants of the National Phenology Network. It asks iNaturalist directly from this browser, about one small request a second (roughly six minutes for the list).

Find trail-wide species ranks every plant in the project by flowering records in its weakest third of the trail (south of 38°N, 38–42°N, north of 42°N): the higher, the more continuously it flowers along the whole trail. Four requests, a few seconds. Flowering south: flowering records south of 38°N (Georgia to southern Virginia). Flowering north: north of 42°N (Massachusetts to Maine). Month bars show flowering records, January to December.
